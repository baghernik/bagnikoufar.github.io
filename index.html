About Me

I am a water resources engineer with a PhD in Civil Engineering – Water Resources Management from the University of Tabriz. For over two decades, I have been at the forefront of water infrastructure management at the East Azerbaijan Regional Water Company (Ministry of Energy, Iran), currently serving as the Head of Operation and Maintenance of Dams and Hydropower Plants.

My career is defined by a dual commitment: managing large-scale physical water systems (dams, hydropower, water/wastewater treatment facilities) and pioneering the use of Artificial Intelligence and data-driven decision support systems to optimize them. I lead a 75-person department, overseeing provincial dam operations, instrumentation, rehabilitation, and water allocation under severe drought conditions.

Driven by the need to solve complex, real-world problems, my research focuses on integrating Z-Number theory, Fuzzy Logic, LSTM networks, and Machine Learning to address uncertainty in reservoir operation and climate adaptation. I am actively seeking global opportunities (such as the UK Global Talent visa) to apply my unique blend of practical engineering leadership and advanced AI research to international water challenges.
